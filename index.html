Our Legacy

A community built on faith, knowledge, discipline and lifelong friendships.

40+

Years of Excellence

Building Character, Inspiring Excellence

Bangwe Nazarene Mission Secondary School has shaped generations of learners through quality education, Christian values, leadership and discipline. Our graduates continue making meaningful contributions across Malawi and around the world.

The Alumni Association exists to reconnect former students, preserve our history, celebrate achievements and support future generations through mentorship, scholarships and school development initiatives.

School History

A journey of growth, faith and academic excellence.

1980s

The Beginning

Bangwe Nazarene Mission Secondary School was established to provide quality education rooted in Christian values and discipline.

1990s

Growing Reputation

Student enrolment increased while the school earned recognition for academic excellence and moral leadership.

2000s

Expanding Opportunities

More graduates pursued careers in medicine, education, engineering, business, ICT and public service.
