Our Legacy

The story behind Bangwe Nazarene Mission Secondary School

A Community Built On Faith, Knowledge & Excellence

Bangwe Nazarene Mission Secondary School has been a foundation of academic excellence, discipline and character development. Through generations of learners, the school has produced individuals who continue making meaningful contributions in Malawi and beyond.

The Alumni Association exists to reconnect former students, celebrate achievements, support current learners and preserve the history of our institution.
